[3] Oncology - High-Grade Glioma - Molecular Drivers and Precision Taxonomy - Review.md

Folder: Review/Oncologiques

The 2018 Capper et al. (Nature) study built a Random Forest classifier with 10,000 binary decision trees trained on the 10,000 most informative CpG methylation probes, with raw scores calibrated via multinomial logistic regression (L2 regularization, threshold ≥0.9), yielding 91 methylation classes (82 tumor + 9 non-neoplastic) with 1.14% cross-validated error across 8 Class Families. In a prospective cohort of 1,155 CNS tumor cases, the classifier produced 76% concordance with histopathology, reclassified 12% of cases, and among the reclassified, 71% underwent a WHO grade change (41% upgraded, 30% downgraded) — demonstrating that methylation-based integrated diagnosis is not supplementary but often primary. The 91 classes organize into five categories relative to WHO entities: Category 1 (direct equivalents, 29 classes), Category 2 (molecular subclasses within one WHO entity, e.g., four medulloblastoma subgroups), Category 3 (grade-blending entities, e.g., IDH-mutant gliomas), Category 4 (cross-WHO-entity classes, e.g., meningioma), and Category 5 (WHO-unrecognized novel entities: HGNET-BCOR, HGNET-MN1 [female-enriched], EFT-CIC, Anaplastic PA, Infantile Hemispheric Glioma); CNV profiles are derived from the same 450k/EPIC array via the conumee Bioconductor package using 100 reference controls.

[4] Exercise Science - Whey Protein - Bolus Absorption vs Muscle Utilization - Review.md

Folder: Review/Gym and Body

The gut absorbs essentially all of a 30–60g whey bolus (near-100% efficiency via gastric emptying modulation); the "30g limit" myth conflates absorption with muscle protein synthesis (MPS) utilization — the actual limiting factor is how much of the absorbed pool bypasses splanchnic first-pass oxidation to reach peripheral skeletal muscle. For a 60g fast bolus (~10 min), ~8–9g is ultimately incorporated into myofibrillar protein over 12 hours; sipping the same dose over 3–4 hours raises this to ~10–12g by sustaining leucine elevation above the mTORC1 threshold longer and reducing leucine oxidation; Trommelen et al. 2023 (Cell Reports Medicine) confirmed 100g milk protein yields ~30% higher MPS over 12h vs. 25g with no true saturation ceiling. Optimal daily distribution is ~0.4 g/kg body mass per meal, 3–5 meals/day spaced by the ~3–4h muscle-full refractory period, plus 30–40g casein pre-sleep (+22–37% overnight MPS); East Asian/Chinese men have lower appendicular lean mass at equivalent BMI, higher ACTN3 R577X null (XX) genotype prevalence (~25% vs. ~18% European, endurance-biased fiber profile), and lower habitual protein intakes, so protein targets should use lean body mass and absolute per-meal doses adjusted accordingly.
