## Mughal Legacy & Indian Nationalism

### [external] [[The Economist - The Mughals - External|The Mughals: What Have They Ever Done for Us?]]
*The Economist* · 2026-04-25

On the 500th anniversary of the Battle of Panipat, the piece interrogates BJP revisionism that frames Mughal rule as a foreign humiliation to be repudiated. Against this, it catalogs the Mughal inheritance that saturates Indian life: Hindi vocabulary, Mughlai cuisine, the Taj Mahal and Red Fort, the sitar, the sherwani, Sufi religious traditions. The argument is pointed — Modi's speeches are delivered in a language substantially built on Persian — and the piece frames Mughal legacy not as a conquest's residue but as a constitutive element of Indian civilization that Hindu nationalism cannot coherently disown without disowning India itself.
