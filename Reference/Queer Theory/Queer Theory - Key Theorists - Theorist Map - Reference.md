| Theorist | Field | Core concepts | Key works | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Judith Butler** | Philosophy / gender studies | Performativity; gender as citational practice; heterosexual matrix; precarity | *Gender Trouble* (1990); *Bodies That Matter* (1993); *Precarious Life* (2004) | Use to argue that gender and sexuality are constituted through repeated performative acts, not expressive of prior essences |
| **Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick** | Literary theory / queer theory | Homosocial desire; epistemology of the closet; universalizing vs minoritizing definitions of homosexuality | *Between Men* (1985); *Epistemology of the Closet* (1990); *Tendencies* (1993) | Use for the closet as a structuring epistemological device in modern Western culture; distinction between minoritizing and universalizing sexual logics |
| **Michael Warner** | Literary and cultural theory | Heteronormativity; publics and counterpublics; sexual shame | *Fear of a Queer Planet* (ed., 1993); *The Trouble with Normal* (1999); *Publics and Counterpublics* (2002) | Use to critique normalizing frameworks even within LGBTQ+ politics; queer counterpublics as sites of stigma-resistant community |
| **Lauren Berlant** | Cultural theory | Cruel optimism; intimate publics; slow death; national sentimentality | *The Queen of America Goes to Washington City* (1997); *The Female Complaint* (2008); *Cruel Optimism* (2011) | Use for attachments to conditions that undermine flourishing; affective dimensions of political life and precarity |
