## Condensed conceptual alignment

| Analytic layer                               | Best theorists       | Core question                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Interactional meaning**                    | Gumperz, Blom, Auer  | What does the switch do here, in this moment?                                                 |
| **Accommodation / interpersonal adjustment** | Giles                | Is the speaker converging, diverging, or maintaining difference?                              |
| **Grammar of mixed speech**                  | Myers-Scotton        | Which language supplies the morphosyntactic frame?                                            |
| **Group and community practice**             | Gardner-Chloros      | How does switching vary by community, subgroup, and repertoire?                               |
| **Institutional power**                      | Heller, Bourdieu     | Which speech forms carry value, legitimacy, or penalty?                                       |
| **Language ideology**                        | Woolard, Silverstein | How do speech forms become signs of authenticity, neutrality, class, ethnicity, or authority? |
| **Colonial subject formation**               | Fanon, Bhabha        | How does colonial language hierarchy shape desire, mimicry, shame, and ambivalence?           |

## Red team review

The table risks flattening real disagreements. Auer’s sequential analysis is not simply a supplement to Gumperz; it can criticize macro-symbolic readings. Myers-Scotton’s models are more formal and rule-oriented than Heller’s or Woolard’s political-economic work. Fanon and Bhabha are not sociolinguists in the narrow sense; they are being used here as language-and-power theorists.

## Best additions for your specific scenario

The most directly useful additions are **Bell, Coupland, Rampton, Eckert, Bucholtz & Hall, Lippi-Green, Irvine & Gal, Agha, Kachru, and Schneider**.

For the **years-abroad** case, prioritize **Labov, Bell, Milroy, Kachru, Schneider, Pavlenko, and Canagarajah**.

For the **weeks-abroad** case, prioritize **Coupland, Rampton, Bell, Goffman, Bucholtz & Hall, Eckert, and Agha**.

For the **listener’s annoyance/offense**, prioritize **Bourdieu from the original table**, then add **Lippi-Green, Woolard, Irvine & Gal, Eckert, Agha, and Tajfel & Turner**.
