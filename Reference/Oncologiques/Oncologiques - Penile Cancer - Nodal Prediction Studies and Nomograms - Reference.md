## Studies Directly Correlating cN with pN

**Chipollini et al. (2016)** is the most explicit study. They specifically calculated **weighted kappa statistics** for the correlation between clinical and pathologic node status across 875 patients with complete cN/pN data. Of 461 cN0 patients undergoing ILND, **24% were upstaged to pN+**, demonstrating that clinical examination substantially underestimates true nodal burden in clinically negative groins.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih

**Graafland et al. (2010)** studied 342 cN0 patients and found **20% had occult metastasis** — again showing that clinical negativity significantly underestimates pathologic disease. They also noted extracapsular extension correlated with clinical nodal status (13% in cN0 vs. 66% in cN+ patients).cjcrcn +1

**Althaf et al. (2016)** from India (N=230) showed that **112/230 (48.6%)** had clinically palpable nodes at presentation, but only **74 of those 112** (66%) had FNAC-confirmed metastasis. This highlights that roughly **one-third of clinically positive nodes are reactive/inflammatory**, not metastatic.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih

## Studies Using cN as a Predictor Variable

**Ficarra et al. (2006)** and **Peak et al. (2019)** both incorporated **clinical nodal status (cN) as a variable** within their nomograms to predict pathologic nodal involvement, and it was one of the strongest predictors in both models (c-indices 0.876 and 0.880, respectively).auajournals +1

**Zhou et al. (2020)** built nomograms specifically for **cN+ patients**, incorporating imaging short-axis diameter of the largest inguinal lymph node, and achieved a c-index of 0.948 for predicting pathologically confirmed ILNM.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih

## The Broader cN vs pN Discordance

The 2025 EAU guidelines summarise the key clinical issue:uroweb

- In **cN0 patients**, micro-metastases cannot be reliably excluded by clinical examination or imaging; approximately **25% harbour occult disease**.
- In **cN+ patients**, nodal metastases are confirmed in approximately **45–80%** of cases, meaning **20–55% of palpable nodes are reactive** (false positives).
- Inguinal US + FNAC has a specificity of 100% but sensitivity of only 39%, so a negative US cannot rule out metastasis.
- CT has a sensitivity of only 20–38% for pelvic LN detection.uroweb

**Graafland et al. (2011)** separately showed that CT findings of **central nodal necrosis and/or irregular borders** identified high-risk pathologic node-positive disease (≥3 positive ILN, ENE, or pelvic mets) with **sensitivity 95% and specificity 82%**.sciencedirect

## Key Takeaway

The consistent finding across these studies is that **clinical nodal assessment alone is unreliable** in both directions — it misses ~25% of occult disease in cN0 patients and overcalls metastasis in ~33–55% of cN+ patients. This is precisely why the field has moved toward DSNB, nomograms, and pathologic risk-stratification tools to better predict true pN status.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
