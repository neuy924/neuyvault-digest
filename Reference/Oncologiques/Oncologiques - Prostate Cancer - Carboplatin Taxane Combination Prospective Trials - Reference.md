## Key Takeaways

The evidence base for carboplatin–taxane combinations in metastatic prostate cancer has evolved substantially over two decades. The early single-arm docetaxel–carboplatin studies (Ross, Aparicio) established proof-of-concept, particularly in docetaxel-refractory and aggressive variant (AVPC) populations. The RECARDO trial—the only randomised docetaxel ± carboplatin study—was negative but was limited by early closure, a docetaxel-sensitive selected population, and relatively low chemotherapy dosing in the combination arm.scholars.duke +4

The pivotal Corn et al. (2019) trial is the landmark study in this space: it is the first randomised trial to demonstrate a statistically significant PFS benefit for adding carboplatin to a taxane (cabazitaxel) in mCRPC, with a 31% reduction in risk of progression (HR 0.69, p=0.018). The benefit appeared enriched in patients meeting aggressive variant clinicopathological criteria (AVPC-C). This led directly to the ongoing phase III CARAVAN trial (S2312), which is stratifying by the AVPC molecular-pathologic signature and is powered for overall survival.healthscout +3

More recently, biomarker-selected trials targeting HRD/BRCA-mutated mCRPC (Cheng et al., PLATIPARP) have shown promising PSA response rates with docetaxel–carboplatin, particularly in patients with BRCA-complex gene alterations, even after prior PARPi exposure. These findings reinforce the concept that molecular selection (AVPC-MS, HRD status) is likely key to identifying the patients most likely to benefit from platinum–taxane combinations.sciencedirect +2
