## Notes

- Published ICI evidence in penile SCC is concentrated in advanced or metastatic disease; no mature neoadjuvant or adjuvant ICI efficacy dataset was identified in the retrieved sources.[cite:1][cite:16][cite:17]
- Combination chemo-immunotherapy appears to produce higher response rates than ICI monotherapy in currently available studies, although cross-trial comparison remains limited by small sample sizes and nonrandomized designs.[cite:16][cite:21][cite:23]
- Duration of response is inconsistently reported across abstracts and retrospective series, so this field is incomplete for several studies.[cite:17][cite:21][cite:23]
