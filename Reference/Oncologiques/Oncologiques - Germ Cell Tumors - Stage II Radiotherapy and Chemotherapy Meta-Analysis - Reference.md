# Meta-analysis

## 13 studies included
- 4 prospective, 9 retrospective 
- 7 studies evaluating RT only 
- 2 evaluating CT only 
- 4 evaluating both RT and CT 

## 890 patients 
- 607 with RT, 283 for CT 

## Time frame 1950-2010
- Variety of RT fields (PA/ipsilateral pelvis, mediastinal, supraclavicular, scrotal) with heterogeneous doses 
- Various CT regimens (mostly BEP or EP, but also HOP (ifosfamide, vincristine, cisplatin), PVB (cisplatin, vinblastine, bleomycin), modified VAB (vinblastine, cyclophosphamide, dactinomycin, bleomycin), and single-agent carboplatin (CBDCA))
