# Pitfalls in Advanced GCT : Slow Terminal Decline of HCG

## Management Strategies and Outcomes of Germ Cell Tumor Patients With Very High Human Chorionic Gonadotropin Levels  
By Robin T. Zon, Craig Nichols, and Lawrence H. Einhorn

- 41 pts w/ HCG > 50,000 at chemo start (4 cycles)
- 2 (5%) with normal HCG at start of C4 chemo 
- 8 (20%) add'l pts w/ normal HCG 1 month post-chemo (total 10)
  
- 31 (76%) w/ elevated HCG at 1 month post-chemo
  - 15 continuously NED w/o further chemo
  - Median time to normal HCG for these 15 pts was 149 days (e.g., 60 days post-chemo)

- 13/41 underwent post-chemo surgery
  - 13 with necrosis only
  - 7 with teratoma only
  - 1 with persistent viable GCT
