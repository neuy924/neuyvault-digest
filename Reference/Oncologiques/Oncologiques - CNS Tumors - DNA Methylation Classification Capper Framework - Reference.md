**Important Notes:**

1. Survival estimates represent median values or ranges compiled from multiple studies and may vary based on treatment protocols, extent of resection, age, and other clinical factors.[4][5][12][20][21][22][23]

2. The molecular features listed are the most characteristic and recurrent alterations but are not universally present in all cases within each methylation class.[1]

3. Methylation-based classification provides more refined prognostic stratification than histology alone, particularly for ambiguous cases.[1]

4. Several classes have limited survival data due to rarity or recent molecular characterization.[1]

5. Survival outcomes for IDH-mutant gliomas are significantly better than IDH-wildtype tumors of similar grade (10-year PFS: 62.5-71.7% for oligodendroglioma vs. 30.4-38.3% for astrocytoma).[4]

6. MGMT promoter methylation status provides additional prognostic information within several classes but shows variable significance across different molecular subtypes.[21][24][25][26]
