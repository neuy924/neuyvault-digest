# Second-Line Systemic Therapy Studies in Adrenocortical Carcinoma

## Overview

Advanced adrenocortical carcinoma (ACC) has no regulatory-approved second-line (2L) regimen; evidence derives from small phase II trials, phase 1b expansion cohorts, and the FIRM-ACT cross-over analysis. The most cited options after failure of EDP-M (etoposide, doxorubicin, cisplatin + mitotane) include gemcitabine + capecitabine/5-FU, streptozotocin + mitotane (cross-over), single-agent immune checkpoint inhibitors (pembrolizumab, avelumab, nivolumab), TKIs (cabozantinib, sunitinib), and combination IO + TKI regimens (cabozantinib + atezolizumab, camrelizumab + apatinib). Current expert reviews favour gemcitabine + capecitabine or combination immunotherapy/TKI where a clinical trial is not available.[^1][^2]

## Synthesis

Across 2L trials, objective response rates remain modest (roughly 6–23% for single-agent IO, ~11% for cabozantinib, ~7% for gemcitabine-fluoropyrimidine) and median PFS is typically 2–6 months, with median OS ranging from ~10 to ~25 months depending on selection and prior therapy burden. A 2025 meta-analysis of prospective trials confirmed these benchmarks and highlighted heterogeneity across modality and line of therapy. Expert reviews recommend gemcitabine + capecitabine or streptozotocin (± mitotane) as reasonable cytotoxic 2L choices, with pembrolizumab (or IO + TKI combinations) preferred in fit patients with MSI-high, Lynch syndrome, or high TMB biology, and clinical trial enrolment encouraged whenever feasible.[^8][^3][^7][^11][^18][^10][^2][^1]
