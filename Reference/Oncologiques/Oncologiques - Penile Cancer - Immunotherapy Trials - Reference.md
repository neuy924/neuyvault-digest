# Penile Cancer Prospective Immunotherapy Trials
### A Comprehensive Tabulated Review Organised by Disease Stage

---

## Background

Penile squamous cell carcinoma (PSCC) is a rare but aggressive malignancy with an incidence of approximately 36,000 cases per year globally. Advanced-stage disease carries a poor prognosis, with 5-year survival rates falling to 9% in the setting of distant metastases. For decades, platinum-based chemotherapy (TIP or cisplatin/5-FU regimens) represented the only systemic option. The rationale for immunotherapy rests on high rates of PD-L1 expression (40-79%), frequent tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes, and HPV-associated oncogenesis in approximately 30-50% of cases. The landscape of immunotherapy trials in PSCC has accelerated substantially since 2018, spanning neoadjuvant, first-line, maintenance, and later-line settings.

## Commentary

### Efficacy Signal Summary

The most meaningful clinical advance has come from chemo-immunotherapy combinations in the first-line setting. The HERCULES trial (pembrolizumab plus cisplatin/5-FU) demonstrated an ORR of 39.4% and a mOS of 9.6 months in advanced PSCC, representing the first prospective trial to show ICI efficacy in this disease. The EPIC-A trial (cemiplimab plus cisplatin/5-FU) further advanced this, reporting a mOS of 15.5 months - the highest recorded in any advanced PSCC trial to date - with a CBR of 62.1%. In the neoadjuvant setting, the toripalimab plus nimotuzumab plus TIP regimen achieved a pCR rate of 48.3% with an 82.8% ORR in locally advanced disease, with 2-year OS of 72.4%.

ICI monotherapy has shown more modest activity. Single-agent nivolumab (AcSe), retifanlimab (ORPHEUS), and avelumab (ALPACA, EPIC-B) each achieved ORRs of 14-17% in unselected advanced PSCC populations. The PERICLES trial combining atezolizumab with radiotherapy failed to meet its primary endpoint (1-yr PFS 12.5% vs target at least 35%), though biomarker analyses identified hrHPV positivity and high intratumoral CD3+CD8+ T-cell infiltration as predictors of benefit.

### Biomarker Insights

Across trials, the following have emerged as enriching biomarkers for ICI response in PSCC:
- HPV16 positivity: ORR 55.6% vs 35% in HPV16-negative patients (HERCULES)
- TMB-High: ORR 75% vs 36.4% TMB-low (HERCULES)
- High intratumoral CD3+CD8+ TILs: Associated with improved PFS in PERICLES (p=0.037)
- MSI-High or TMB-High (approximately 1% of PSCC): Durable responses in multiple case series
- PD-L1 CPS expression: Inconsistent predictor across PSCC trials; paradoxically higher ORR in CPS 0 vs CPS >=1 in HERCULES (66.7% vs 33.3%)

### Ongoing Priorities

The PRIAM trial (NCT06353906) will define the role of neoadjuvant carboplatin/paclitaxel plus pembrolizumab in resectable locoregionally advanced PSCC. The BRAVA PENILE trial represents the first randomised design in PSCC immunotherapy, directly addressing whether ICI maintenance adds PFS benefit over observation after first-line chemotherapy. Novel mechanisms are under exploration in NCT05526989 (PARP inhibitor plus PD-1) and SMART (Trop2 ADC plus PD-L1).

### Trial Design Considerations

The rarity of PSCC has driven reliance on single-arm phase II designs with modest sample sizes. International collaboration, as exemplified by ICONIC, ORPHEUS, and HERCULES, is essential. The 2024 EAU-ASCO clinical practice guidelines now recommend ICI consideration in MSI-High and TMB-High PSCC, and note that pembrolizumab plus platinum-based chemotherapy may represent a new first-line option based on HERCULES and EPIC-A data.
