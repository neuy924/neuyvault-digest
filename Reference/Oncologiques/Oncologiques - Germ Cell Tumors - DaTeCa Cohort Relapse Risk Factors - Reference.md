## 2. Seminoma Results (2024)

**Prognostic Factors for Relapse in Patients With Clinical Stage I Testicular Seminoma: A Nationwide, Population-Based Cohort Study**

Wagner T, et al.
*Journal of Clinical Oncology* 2024 Jan 1;42(1):81–89. *(Epub 2023 Sep 8)*

### Key Findings
- Independent predictors of relapse: hilar invasion (rete testis and hilar soft tissue), lymphovascular invasion (LVI), elevated pre-orchiectomy β-hCG and LDH
- Estimated 5-year relapse risk: **6%** (no risk factors) to **62%** (all four risk factors with hilar soft tissue invasion)
- C-index: 0.70 (corrected for optimism)
- Replaces the Warde model (tumour size >4 cm + rete testis invasion) with a four-factor risk model

## 3. Non-Seminoma Results (2024)

**Prognostic factors for relapse in patients with clinical stage I testicular non-seminoma: A nationwide, population-based cohort study**

Wagner T, Toft BG, Lauritsen J, et al.
*European Journal of Cancer* 2024 May;202:114025. *(Epub 2024 Mar 17)*

### Key Findings
- Independent predictors of relapse: hilar soft tissue invasion, tumour size, LVI, and embryonal carcinoma
- Estimated 5-year relapse risk: **<5%** to **>85%** depending on number of risk factors
- C-index: 0.75 with excellent calibration
- Provides substantially more accurate risk stratification than current LVI-only clinical practice

### Links
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38531266/
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.ejca.2024.114025
