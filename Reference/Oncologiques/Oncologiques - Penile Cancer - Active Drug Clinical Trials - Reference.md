## Notes

- **Stage nomenclature** reflects the study-specific staging described in the trial protocol; local staging systems (e.g., node-positive, locoregionally advanced) are used where formal TNM staging was not cited.
- **N values** represent estimated enrolment unless noted as "actual" (i.e., achieved).
- Trials listed as **Not Yet Recruiting** are registered and planned to open.
- Several trials (Sections B and C) are basket designs enrolling multiple histologies; the overall N includes all cohorts, not penile-specific enrolment.
- HPV vaccine prevention trials (NCT06866574, NCT06465914), penile rehabilitation studies (NCT06875258, NCT06717711), and observational/natural history studies have been excluded.
- **LATENT (NCT03357757)** has been excluded as its status was updated to **Completed** at time of data extraction.
