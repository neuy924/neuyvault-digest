## Summary and Clinical Context

**Current standard of care.** Based on the landmark KEYNOTE-048 results, pembrolizumab monotherapy (for PD-L1 CPS ≥1) or pembrolizumab plus platinum/5-FU (regardless of CPS) are the established 1L regimens. The 5-year follow-up data published in 2025 (Tahara et al.) reinforce long-term benefit, with ~19% of pembrolizumab monotherapy patients and ~20% of pembrolizumab-chemotherapy patients alive at 5 years.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +2

**Negative phase 3 trials.** Both CheckMate 651 (nivolumab/ipilimumab) and KESTREL (durvalumab ± tremelimumab) failed to demonstrate OS superiority over EXTREME in their primary analyses, underscoring the difficulty of improving on pembrolizumab-based strategies and highlighting that dual immunotherapy without a chemotherapy backbone is insufficient in the unselected population.oncnursingnews +1

**Alternative chemotherapy backbones.** The TPExtreme trial demonstrated that docetaxel/cisplatin/cetuximab is a viable and better-tolerated alternative to EXTREME, although it did not achieve OS superiority. KEYNOTE-B10 supports carboplatin/paclitaxel as a practical 5-FU–free backbone when combined with pembrolizumab, addressing the logistical challenges of 5-FU continuous infusion.icm.unicancer +1

**Emerging chemotherapy-free strategies.** Phase 2 data from Chung et al. and Sacco et al. show promising activity for anti-PD-1 + cetuximab combinations without cytotoxic chemotherapy, with impressive median OS (18–20 months) and favourable toxicity profiles. These results provide a rationale for randomised phase 3 evaluation of chemotherapy-sparing immunotherapy–targeted therapy combinations, particularly for patients who may not tolerate intensive chemotherapy.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

## The Pivotal Comparison: Kish et al. 1985

The only randomised trial directly comparing bolus versus infusional 5-FU in combination with cisplatin in HNSCC was conducted at Wayne State University. Kish et al. randomised 44 patients with recurrent/advanced HNSCC to cisplatin 100 mg/m² D1 + 5-FU 1000 mg/m²/day as a 24-hour continuous infusion D1–4 versus cisplatin 100 mg/m² D1 + **bolus 5-FU 600 mg/m² D1 and D8**. Among 38 evaluable patients:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih

- **Infusion arm:** ORR 72% (4 CR + 9 PR out of 18)
- **Bolus arm:** ORR 20% (2 CR + 2 PR out of 20) — p < 0.01

The bolus arm was associated with significantly worse myelosuppression (grade 3/4 leukopenia in 70%, thrombocytopenia in 50%), whereas stomatitis was more common but mild and reversible in the infusion arm. Complete responders on either arm had median survival of 120+ weeks, but partial responders only 30 weeks. This trial established continuous-infusion cisplatin/5-FU as the standard backbone and effectively eliminated bolus 5-FU from further investigation in this disease.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih

## Why Infusional 5-FU Became the Standard in HNSCC

The pharmacological rationale is well established. Bolus and infusional 5-FU behave almost as "two different drugs":cancernetwork

- **Bolus 5-FU** is primarily incorporated into RNA, producing cytotoxicity through RNA processing disruption. It causes more myelosuppression and stomatitis.
- **Infusional 5-FU** primarily inhibits thymidylate synthase (TS), blocking DNA synthesis. It has a different toxicity profile (hand-foot syndrome, mucositis) but less haematotoxicity.

In HNSCC, the TS-inhibitory mechanism of prolonged drug exposure appears to be more therapeutically active. Every subsequent major trial adopted the infusional schedule:

## Meirovitz et al. 2021 (Retrospective, Modified Bi-weekly Regimen)

A retrospective series of 27 patients with R/M HNSCC treated with a modified EXTREME-type protocol using **bolus 5-FU + leucovorin on D1** combined with a shortened 48-hour continuous infusion of 5-FU (D1–2), plus cisplatin and cetuximab, on a biweekly cycle. ## Clinical Implications

The evidence overwhelmingly favours continuous-infusion 5-FU when combined with platinum in HNSCC. If the clinical concern driving this question is the **logistical burden of infusional 5-FU** (ambulatory pumps, central venous access, hospitalisation), there are more evidence-based alternatives than switching to bolus 5-FU:

- **Replace 5-FU entirely with a taxane:** The TPExtreme trial (Guigay, *Lancet Oncol* 2021) demonstrated that docetaxel/cisplatin/cetuximab is a viable 5-FU–free regimen with comparable OS and better tolerability.sciencedirect
- **Replace 5-FU with carboplatin/paclitaxel:** KEYNOTE-B10 (Dzienis, *JCO* 2024) prospectively validated pembrolizumab + carboplatin + paclitaxel as a practical infusion-free alternative, with ORR 49% and mOS 13.1 months.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih
- **Pembrolizumab monotherapy:** For CPS ≥1 patients, single-agent pembrolizumab avoids chemotherapy entirely and has superior long-term survival in CPS ≥20.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih

In summary, bolus 5-FU + platinum has been tested in HNSCC but shown to be clearly inferior (ORR 20% vs. 72% in the Kish trial), and no subsequent trial has revisited this approach at scale. The current evidence base does not support bolus 5-FU as a substitute for infusional 5-FU when used with platinum in this disease.

## Practical interpretation

- **Efficacy:** Cisplatin is associated with **better long-term OS**, particularly in non-nasopharyngeal SCCHN, in pooled analyses and some R/M trials.sciencedirect +1
- **Toxicity:**
	- Cisplatin: more nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, nausea/vomiting, neuropathy.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
	- Carboplatin: more haematologic toxicity (anaemia, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia), but generally easier in patients with renal impairment or frailty.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
- **Guideline-consistent use:**
	- Use **cisplatin** (weekly 40 mg/m² or 100 mg/m² q3w) as first choice in fit patients for definitive and postoperative CRT.
	- Use **carboplatin (often with paclitaxel or 5‑FU)** when cisplatin is contraindicated (GFR <60, hearing loss, neuropathy, frailty), recognising that survival is probably slightly inferior but still better than RT alone or RT+cetuximab.frontiersin +1
