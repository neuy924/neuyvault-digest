# Systematic Review of GCT Relapse After RT

## 2/7 studies included relapse rates
- For IIA: RR for RT was 0%–10.3% and CT was 0%
- For IIB: RR for RT was 28.6% and CT was 0%
  - Driven by a single retrospective study where 11/30 patients treated for IIA-C disease (mostly IIB) relapsed, and 8/30 patients treated with chemotherapy for IIA-C disease relapsed (mostly IIC)

## 2/7 studies reported relapse free survival
- For II (including IIA-C), 5-year RFS 92% for RT and 96% for CT
- 5-year RFS 88.7% for RT (IIA only) and 100% for CT (IIA/B)
