## PCNSL. Non-germinal center. What does it mean and significance

In PCNSL, “non‑germinal center” is essentially a **cell‑of‑origin (COO) label**, and it means:

- The lymphoma immunophenotype is **non‑germinal center B‑cell (non‑GCB)** by Hans (or similar) algorithm
- Biologically, this corresponds to an **activated B‑cell‑like (ABC‑like), late germinal‑center/early post‑germinal‑center B cell**, not a classic germinal‑center B cell
- In PCNSL, this is the **rule, not the exception**

Below is what that means and why it matters.

---

## 1\. What “non‑germinal center” means in PCNSL

For DLBCL (and by extension PCNSL), COO is commonly classified as:

- **GCB type** (germinal center B‑cell–like)
- **Non‑GCB / ABC type** (activated B‑cell–like)

Using IHC (Hans algorithm), non‑GCB PCNSL typically shows:

- **CD10 negative**
- **BCL6 variable**
- **MUM1/IRF4 positive**

Several large series show that **the majority (roughly 70–90%) of primary CNS DLBCL are non‑GCB/ABC by Hans or similar algorithms**. Molecular profiling (GEP) confirms that most PCNSL cluster with **ABC‑like DLBCL** rather than GCB‑like.aol.amegroups +3

Pathobiologically:

- PCNSL tumor cells derive from **“germinal‑center–exit” B cells**, i.e., late GC/early post‑GC cells, rather than naïve or centroblastic GCB cells.ruralneuropractice +1
- They almost uniformly express an **activated B‑cell phenotype** (CD10⁻/BCL6±/MUM1⁺).pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih

So “PCNSL, non‑germinal center” on a report is basically saying:

> This PCNSL has an **activated B‑cell–like, non‑GCB cell of origin**, which is the typical phenotype for PCNSL.

## 2\. Molecular features that go with the non‑GCB phenotype

Non‑GCB/ABC‑like PCNSL has a characteristic genetic profile:

- Very high frequency of **MYD88 L265P** mutations and **CD79B** mutations, placing most cases in the **MCD/C5 genetic cluster**, analogous to ABC DLBCL with extranodal tropism.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
- Frequent mutations in **PIM1, KMT2D** and other NF‑κB–pathway and BCR‑signaling genes.haematologica +1
- Gene‑expression profiling shows high expression of **activation/BCR pathway genes** (e.g., BTK, PLCG2, CXCR4) and relatively low expression of classical GCB markers.ascopubs

These alterations converge on **chronic active BCR signaling and NF‑κB activation**, which is biologically consistent with an **activated, non‑GCB cell of origin**.aol.amegroups +1

## 3\. Prognostic significance in PCNSL

Unlike nodal DLBCL, where GCB vs ABC has clear prognostic separation with R‑CHOP, the situation in PCNSL is more nuanced:

- Most large PCNSL cohorts are **overwhelmingly non‑GCB**, so data on true GCB‑type PCNSL are limited.ashpublications +1
- In a Japanese series of 42 primary CNS B‑cell lymphomas, **71% were non‑GCB and 29% GCB by Hans**. GCB cases had a **trend toward better overall survival** versus non‑GCB, and patients with **GCB / MYD88‑wild‑type** had the best outcomes.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih
- Another study reported that **GCB‑type PCNSL had markedly better survival (all alive at ~22‑month median follow‑up) compared with non‑GCB, which had a median OS around 11 months**.sciencedirect

So:

- **Non‑GCB is the standard phenotype** in PCNSL and is associated with the generally poor prognosis typical of PCNSL as a disease.
- **GCB‑type, when present, may be a favorable prognostic subset**, but these cases are uncommon.

At present, COO is **not yet a formally incorporated prognostic index** in PCNSL the way it is in nodal DLBCL, but emerging data suggest it has **prognostic signal**, especially when combined with MYD88 status.sciencedirect +1

## 4\. Therapeutic implications

In daily practice:

- Front‑line treatment of PCNSL (HD‑MTX–based regimens ± rituximab, consolidation with ASCT or WBRT) **does not change** based on GCB vs non‑GCB status; all are treated as aggressive PCNSL.cancer +1
- However, the non‑GCB/ABC biology has several **therapeutic implications and research directions**:
	- Dependence on **chronic active BCR signaling and NF‑κB** (via MYD88/CD79B, BTK, PLCG2) provides a rationale for **BTK inhibitors (e.g., ibrutinib)** and other pathway‑targeted agents in PCNSL.ascopubs +1
	- The same non‑GCB signature is associated with **CNS tropism** in systemic DLBCL; non‑GCB, MYC/BCL2 overexpression, and MCD‑like genetics enrich for cases that can seed the CNS.haematologica

So, while “non‑germinal center” **currently does not alter standard front‑line management** of PCNSL, it:

- Confirms that the tumor fits the **typical PCNSL biology** (ABC‑like, MCD‑type)
- May carry a **less favorable prognosis** compared with the rare GCB‑type PCNSL
- Points toward **targetable signaling pathways** that are being exploited in trials (BTK inhibitors, etc.)

## Summary of the Evidence

## PCNSL: mostly conflicting data, but an emerging signal

The vast majority (70–96%) of PCNSL are **non-GCB/ABC-like** by Hans algorithm, making GCB-type PCNSL a rare subgroup with very small numbers across all studies. This fundamental limitation makes it difficult to detect a survival difference even when one may exist.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

- **Two studies found GCB to be a favorable prognostic factor:**
	- **Marcus et al. (2021, Harvard/BIDMC)** — 24 patients: all GCB-type alive at 22-month median follow-up; non-GCB median OS only 11 months.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih
	- **Oka et al. (2025, Fukushima)** — 42 patients: GCB had significantly better OS (P = 0.023) and PFS (P = 0.019) on univariate analysis. After adjusting for age and R-MPV therapy, a favorable **trend** persisted (HR 0.32, 95% CI 0.07–1.45). The best-outcome subgroup was GCB/MYD88-wild-type.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih
- **The majority of earlier (and larger) studies found no significant difference:**
	- **Villa et al. (2019, BC Cancer)** — the largest single-center series (n = 115) using tissue microarray: ≥75% non-GCB; COO did not affect PFS or OS.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih
	- Multiple other cohorts (Liu 2017, Raoux 2010, Lin 2006, Camilleri-Broët 2006, Aki 2013, Hattab 2010, Momota 2010) uniformly failed to reach statistical significance, though nearly all had extremely few GCB cases (often <10).pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih
- **Interpretation:** The studies that *did* find a difference tend to be more recent, used better-defined treatment protocols (R-MPV), and integrated MYD88 mutational status. The older "negative" studies had heterogeneous treatments, fewer GCB cases, and did not integrate molecular data. The emerging consensus is that there is likely a **real but modest prognostic signal** from COO in PCNSL, especially when combined with MYD88 status, but this has not reached the level of a validated prognostic marker.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

## Systemic nodal DLBCL: GCB vs ABC is a well-established prognostic factor

In systemic DLBCL treated with R-CHOP, **GEP-defined GCB subtype has a clearly superior prognosis** (5-year PFS ~75% vs ~40% for ABC). However, the **Hans IHC algorithm** — which is the tool typically used in PCNSL studies — has only ~70–80% concordance with GEP and often fails to reach significance for OS separation.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +3

## Immune-privileged site lymphomas (IP-LBCL: CNS, testis, vitreoretinal)

The 2024 WHO-5th edition groups PCNSL, primary testicular DLBCL, and primary vitreoretinal lymphoma (PVRL) as **IP-LBCL**. All share an overwhelmingly non-GCB/ABC phenotype with MYD88 L265P / CD79B co-mutations (MCD genetic subtype), which explains their shared CNS tropism and aggressive biology. Primary testicular DLBCL similarly shows non-GCB predominance with poor outcomes and a high relapse rate at extranodal/CNS sites.sciencedirect +2

## Key Takeaways

1. **In systemic DLBCL**, GCB vs ABC is a validated prognostic factor (GCB = better). The strongest separation is seen with gene expression profiling; IHC (Hans) is less reliable.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
2. **In PCNSL**, most studies (especially those prior to 2020) found **no significant difference**, but this is almost certainly driven by extremely small GCB subgroups and heterogeneous treatments. The two most recent studies with better molecular characterization (Oka 2025, Marcus 2021) **both found GCB to be favorable**, and Oka showed that combining GCB status with MYD88 status further refines prognosis.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
3. **All immune-privileged site lymphomas** (CNS, testis, vitreoretinal) share non-GCB/MCD biology and poor prognosis relative to systemic DLBCL, which may partially explain why comparing COO subtypes within PCNSL has been difficult — the denominator is overwhelmingly non-GCB.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih
4. **Clinical implication today**: COO does not currently alter front-line treatment of PCNSL (HD-MTX–based regimens for all), but it **may** become relevant for risk stratification and for rational use of targeted agents (BTK inhibitors, IMiDs) directed against the ABC/NF-κB biology that dominates this disease.haematologica +1
