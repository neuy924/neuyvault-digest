## Key Notes:

## Clear Cell RCC:

- **CheckMate 214** was the first IO-IO combination showing OS benefit in intermediate/poor-risk patients
- **KEYNOTE-426, CheckMate 9ER, and CLEAR** all demonstrated superior efficacy with IO-TKI combinations across all IMDC risk groups
- **CLEAR** showed the highest ORR (71%) and longest PFS (23.9 months) among IO-TKI trials
- **JAVELIN Renal 101** did not meet the pre-specified significance threshold for OS

## Non-Clear Cell RCC:

- **KEYNOTE-B61** is the largest prospective trial specifically in nccRCC, showing 51% ORR with Len+Pembro
- **SUNNIFORECAST** is the first randomized trial comparing IO-IO vs TKI in nccRCC
- Evidence for nccRCC is more limited compared to ccRCC; most patients were included as subgroups in ccRCC trials
- Papillary and unclassified subtypes appear more responsive to IO therapy than chromophobe
