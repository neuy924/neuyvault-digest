## How to Statistically Consolidate the Evidence

## 1\. Structured evidence synthesis

- Perform a systematic review focused on predefined factors: MGMT, IDH, age, KPS, EOR, multifocality/volume, sex, RT, TMZ, and blood markers (NLR, PLR, LMR, AFR).
- Extract adjusted HRs/ORs with 95% CIs from multivariable Cox or logistic models, recording population (newly diagnosed vs recurrent), WHO classification, treatment (Stupp vs other), and whether IDH‑wt only.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +3

## 2\. Meta-analysis of effect sizes

- Use random‑effects meta-analysis (e.g., DerSimonian–Laird or REML) on log‑HRs for each factor, stratified by:
	- IDH‑wt vs mixed cohorts,
	- Extent of resection categories (biopsy/STR/GTR/supratotal),
	- Treatment era (pre‑ vs post‑Stupp).
- Quantify heterogeneity with $I^2$ and $\tau^2$ , and use meta‑regression to test whether study-level variables (median age, RT/chemo usage, follow‑up length) explain heterogeneity.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

## 3\. Hierarchical/integrated prognostic modeling

- Build a multi‑level Cox model using pooled individual patient data (if obtainable from consortia such as TCGA, CGGA, RTOG, and large institutional cohorts).
- Include patient-level covariates (MGMT, IDH, age, KPS, EOR, RT, TMZ, blood scores, VEGFA/PTEN/TMB) with center as a random effect to account for practice variation.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +3
- Evaluate non‑linear effects (e.g., age, tumor volume) via restricted cubic splines and test interactions (e.g., MGMT × TMZ; age × frailty; EOR × eloquent location).pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

## 4\. Nomogram recalibration and external validation

- Take existing nomograms (Gittleman, Li, Rao, Ferguson, Kudulaiti, newer models) and:
	- Refit them in a common IPD dataset;
	- Compare discrimination (C‑index, time‑dependent AUC) and calibration (calibration plots at 6, 12, 24 months);
	- Use net reclassification improvement and decision‑curve analysis to assess clinical utility.frontiersin +4
- Consider a stacked or ensemble model: combine predictions from several validated nomograms plus a flexible learner (e.g., penalized Cox, gradient boosting survival trees) to improve overall performance while retaining interpretability.

## 5\. Handling emerging and correlated markers

- For correlated predictors (e.g., NLR, PLR, LMR, AFR, VEGFA), use penalized methods (LASSO, elastic net) or principal component analysis to derive composite indices before inclusion in Cox models.frontiersin +1
- Apply internal validation (bootstrap or repeated cross‑validation) to quantify optimism, then perform external validation on independent cohorts (different centers, countries, or later time periods).
