## Systemic Therapies for Meningioma

Meningiomas are the most common primary CNS tumours, and while most are managed with surgery and/or radiotherapy, a subset—particularly WHO grade 2 (atypical) and grade 3 (anaplastic) tumours—recur despite local treatments. No systemic agent is approved as standard of care for refractory meningioma, but over 35 years of clinical trials have evaluated numerous drug classes. The table below synthesises the key efficacy and safety data from completed prospective trials.

## Summary of Key Findings

**Anti-VEGF therapies** (bevacizumab, sunitinib, vatalanib) have consistently shown the strongest signals, with PFS-6 rates of 42–87% depending on grade. A 2025 RANO group meta-analysis of all systemic therapy trials found pooled PFS-6 rates of ~44% for grade 1 and ~38% for grade 2–3 recurrent meningiomas, and recommended new benchmarks of 67% (grade 1) and 49% (grade 2–3) for future trials to declare efficacy.baptisthealth.elsevierpure +1

**CDK4/6 inhibition with abemaciclib** is the most notable recent advance. The Alliance A071401 umbrella trial reported a PFS-6 of 58%, median PFS of 10 months (12.1 months for NF2-mutant tumours), and median OS of 29.1 months in molecularly selected grade 2/3 meningiomas—the first genomically driven positive trial in this disease.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

**Immune checkpoint inhibitors** (pembrolizumab, nivolumab) showed modest activity overall (PFS-6 ~42–48%) but durable responses in rare patients with high tumour mutational burden.nature +1

**Peptide receptor radionuclide therapy (PRRT)** with ¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE achieved a PFS-6 of ~80% in refractory meningiomas in a phase 2 trial, greatly exceeding historical benchmarks.targetedonc +1

Conversely, **mifepristone** (the only completed phase III RCT) showed no benefit over placebo, and **trabectedin** (the first randomised phase II in grade 2/3 tumours) was inferior to local standard of care with greater toxicity.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

## Interpretation and Current Landscape

Several points emerge from the evidence:

- **No agent is standard of care.** The only completed phase III trial (mifepristone) was negative, and the only randomised phase II (trabectedin vs LOC) showed no benefit.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
- **Anti-angiogenic agents** remain the most consistently active class. Bevacizumab monotherapy achieves PFS-6 of 46–87% depending on grade, with median PFS in excess of 15 months for lower-grade tumours. The EORTC-1320-BTG trial's exploratory analysis of LOC patients receiving bevacizumab supports further prospective evaluation.ascopubs +1
- **Molecularly targeted therapy is a paradigm shift.** The Alliance A071401 trial is the first genomically driven study to report positive results, demonstrating that selecting patients by NF2 or CDK pathway alterations identifies a responsive population for abemaciclib. Exploratory analyses suggest NF2-altered patients derive the greatest benefit (median PFS 12.1 months).targetedonc +2
- **PRRT is an emerging modality.** ¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE leverages the high somatostatin receptor expression in meningiomas and achieved a PFS-6 of ~80%—far exceeding historical benchmarks. A randomised trial (LUMEN-1) is now underway.clinicaltrials +1
- **Immunotherapy benefits a niche subset.** Pembrolizumab and nivolumab each achieved PFS-6 above the 26% historical benchmark, but responses were concentrated in patients with high TMB or mismatch repair deficiency—a rare finding in meningioma (~0.6%).pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1

Future directions include ongoing trials of cabozantinib (VEGFR/MET inhibitor), trametinib + alpelisib (MEK + PI3K), vistusertib (TORC1/2), brigatinib (ALK), and combination immunotherapy–radiation approaches.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih

## Practical Interpretation

- For **WHO grade I** meningiomas (especially skull‑base or surgically difficult sites), both SRS and FSRT consistently achieve 5‑year LC or PFS above 85–90% with low permanent toxicity when appropriate doses (e.g. 12–16 Gy single‑fraction SRS or 50–54 Gy FSRT) are used.e-neurofunction +2
- In **atypical (grade II)** tumours, multiple series and the EORTC 22042‑26042 phase II trial support adjuvant RT to 60 Gy after GTR, with 3‑year PFS approaching 90% in modern series; dose ≥60 Gy appears superior to <54–57 Gy.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
- For **anaplastic (grade III)** meningiomas, local control with standard doses is poor; dose escalation with photons or protons above 60 Gy shows better local control but remains under prospective evaluation.academic.oup +1
- **SRS as salvage** for recurrent meningioma is effective, particularly for grade I lesions after surgery alone (LC 79–100%, 5‑yr PFS 40–91%), but outcomes are significantly worse if the tumour has previously received RT.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih +1
