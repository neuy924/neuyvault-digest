## Key Takeaways

- **EDP + Mitotane** remains the only regimen supported by a phase III RCT (FIRM-ACT) as first-line therapy for advanced ACC, with an ORR of 23% and mPFS of 5 months.nebennierenkarzinom
- **Linsitinib** was the other completed phase III trial and was definitively negative, with no OS or PFS benefit over placebo.research.birmingham.ac
- **ADIUVO** is the only adjuvant RCT and showed no significant benefit for mitotane in low-intermediate risk patients.thelancet
- Among **immunotherapy** trials, pembrolizumab (Raj et al.) showed the highest ORR at 23% with remarkable mOS of 24.9 months, though mPFS was short at 2.1 months, suggesting a subset of durable responders.ascopubs
- **Cabozantinib monotherapy** is the most promising targeted agent, with a 4-month PFS rate of 76% and mPFS of 6 months — notably requiring mitotane washout to avoid pharmacokinetic interactions.papers.ssrn +1
- Multiple **ongoing trials** (ACCOMPLISH: pembrolizumab + lenvatinib; cemiplimab maintenance; cabozantinib + cemiplimab; cabozantinib + pembrolizumab) are actively enrolling and may shift the treatment landscape.ascopubs +2
